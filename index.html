Why This Matters

New Mexico uses Daylight Saving Time for about 8 months each year and Standard Time for only 4 months.

That means the clock is moved forward for most of the year.

People who like more evening daylight get that benefit for most of the year. People who prefer more morning daylight only get it for a short time.

We believe one group should not get the benefit for most of the year while the other group gets only a few months.

One clock. All year. That's our goal.

Why We Support Standard Time

Many students, teachers, parents, commuters, and early-morning workers benefit from more morning daylight.

We believe New Mexico should use one simple time all year instead of changing clocks twice every year.

A single year-round clock would be easier, more consistent, and fairer for everyone.

About This Effort

This is a grassroots effort by New Mexicans who support Permanent Mountain Standard Time and want to end the twice-yearly clock changes.

Our goal is to build support, educate the public, and work with state lawmakers to keep New Mexico on one time all year.
