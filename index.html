Our Argument:

New Mexico advances the clock for approximately eight months each year but returns to Standard Time for only four months. In practice, Daylight Saving Time has become the default while Standard Time has become the exception. This means those who prefer later evening daylight receive the benefit of a shifted clock for most of the year, while those who prefer more morning daylight experience Standard Time only during the shortest and darkest months. We believe New Mexicans deserve one fair, balanced, and consistent standard for everyone.
