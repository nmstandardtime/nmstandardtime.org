Why We Believe Change Is Needed

New Mexico moves the clock forward for about 8 months every year but uses Standard Time for only 4 months.

That means Daylight Saving Time is used most of the year, while Standard Time is only used during the shortest and darkest part of the year.

People who like more evening daylight get the benefit for most of the year. People who prefer more morning daylight, including students, teachers, parents, commuters, and early-morning workers, only get that benefit for a short time.

We believe a fair system should not favor one group over another for most of the year.

That's why we support Permanent Mountain Standard Time: one clock, all year, with no more time changes.

About This Petition

This is a grassroots effort by New Mexicans who support Permanent Mountain Standard Time and want to end twice-yearly clock changes.

Our goal is to build public support, engage state legislators, and pursue future legislation that would keep New Mexico on a single, year-round time standard.
